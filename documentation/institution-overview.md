## 3. Users of the System

### Administrator

Responsible for:

- Student records
- Faculty records
- Departments
- Attendance
- Fees
- Examination records
- Reports

### Faculty

Responsible for:

- Viewing assigned students
- Recording attendance
- Entering examination marks
- Viewing academic information

### Management

Mainly uses the system for:

- Enrollment information
- Attendance overview
- Fee collection
- Examination performance
- Faculty workload
- Department-level reports

## 5. Questions the System Should Answer

### Student

- How many students are enrolled?
- How many students are in each department?
- How many students joined this year?
- How many students are active/inactive?

### Faculty

- How many faculty members are there?
- How many faculty belong to each department?
- What is the faculty workload?

### Attendance

- What is the overall attendance percentage?
- Which departments have low attendance?
- Which students have attendance below 75%?

### Fees

- How much fee has been collected?
- How much fee is outstanding?
- Which students have pending fees?

### Examination

- What is the overall pass percentage?
- What is the department-wise performance?
- Which subjects have lower performance?

### Management

- What is the current overall status of the institution?
- Which areas require attention?
